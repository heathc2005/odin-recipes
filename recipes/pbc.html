Peanut Butter Cookies

Home

Description

An easy peanut butter cookies recipes made with only 3 ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1 cup peanut butter
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1 egg

Steps

  1. Gather the ingredients, preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Line baking sheets with parchment paper.
  2. Mix peanut butter, white sugar, and egg
  3. Roll mixture into 1-inch balls and place 1 inch apart in baking sheets.
  4. Bake in the preheated oven until cookies are just barely brown on the bottoms, about 6 to 8 minutes. Cool on the baking sheets briefly before removing to a wire rack to cool completely.
  5. Serve and enjoy!
